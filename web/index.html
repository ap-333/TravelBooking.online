Common questions

Questions, answered.

Can I speak to someone about booking a flight?

Yes. Call us, tell us where you're going and when, and we'll help you review your options.

Do you help with international flights?

Yes. We help you review international options and explain the fare, conditions and booking process.

Can you help with last-minute bookings?

We can help you review what's available at short notice. Availability and fares change quickly and can't be guaranteed.

Do you charge booking assistance fees?

Any fee is explained before you proceed and listed on our Service Fees page.

How do I pay for my flight?

We explain how payment works and who receives it before you decide to proceed.

Who issues my flight ticket?

The airline or an authorized ticketing partner. We tell you which applies to your booking.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

That depends on the airline's fare rules and our policies. See our Cancellation and Refund Policy.

Are you affiliated with any airline?

No. We are an independent service, not affiliated with or endorsed by any airline unless expressly stated.
