Privacy policy

Information we collect

Names, phone numbers and email addresses, travel details such as routes, dates and passenger information, payment-related information handled by our payment processor (primarily PayPal), call recordings, and website usage data.

Call recordings

We record customer calls. We tell you at the start of a call that it may be recorded. Recordings are used for quality assurance, customer support, staff training, dispute resolution and compliance.

How we use it

To search for and book flights, take payment, provide support, meet legal obligations, prevent fraud and improve our service and advertising.

Cookies and similar technologies

Essential cookies and storage keep the site working and remember your choices. With your permission we also use:

  • Analytics to understand how the site is used.
  • Advertising and profiling to measure ad performance, build audience profiles and improve our campaigns, including through Google advertising services.

Non-essential cookies stay off until you accept them. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a refusal of advertising and profiling.

Who we share it with

Airlines and authorized ticketing partners, payment processors such as PayPal, advertising and analytics providers (only with your consent), and service providers that help us run the business.

Your choices

Depending on your state, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information or opt out of its sale or sharing. See Do not sell or share or email contact@travelbooking.online.
