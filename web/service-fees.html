Service fees

Clear pricing, explained before you proceed.

We may charge a nominal assistance or service fee for helping with flight searches, reservations, ticket bookings and related services. The fee that applies is communicated to you before you confirm a booking.

When the fee is refunded

We refund our assistance fee in full (100%) where we are directly responsible for the error, meaning a wrong booking or service request that happened because of our mistake even though you gave us the correct information.

We are not liable, and the fee is not refunded, where the problem comes from wrong or incomplete information supplied by the customer, such as a misspelled name, wrong dates or the wrong airport.

Each case is reviewed on its own facts. See our cancellation and refund policy.

Commissions

We may receive commissions, incentives or other commercial benefits from airlines or authorized travel partners for eligible bookings. Any customer-facing service fee or additional charge is explained before the booking is confirmed.

You are told the total payable amount, including applicable service charges and airline fees, before you make payment.
