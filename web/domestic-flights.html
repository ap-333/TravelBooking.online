FAQ

Common questions

Can I book a domestic flight by phone?

Yes. Call us, share your trip details and we'll help you review options and move the booking forward.

Do you charge booking assistance fees?

Any assistance fee is explained on the call before you decide to proceed, and set out on our Service Fees page. You will see the fare, conditions and any fee before anything is booked.

Who issues my flight ticket?

Tickets are issued by the airline or an authorized ticketing partner. We explain which applies to your booking before you proceed.

Are you affiliated with any airline?

No. We are an independent booking assistance service and are not affiliated with or endorsed by any airline unless expressly stated.
